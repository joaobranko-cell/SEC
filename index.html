01 — The opportunity

Four centuries of continuity.
One strategic asset.

Quinta dos Espadeiros is a rare surviving 17th-century recreational and agricultural estate located in Laranjeiro, Almada — on the south bank of the Tagus, approximately 15 minutes from Lisbon city centre and 18 minutes from the international airport.

The property combines an architectural ensemble of genuine historic character with mature formal gardens, extensive water features and substantial private grounds. The perimeter is planted with trees of Australian origin that form a natural acoustic barrier, effectively shielding the estate from surrounding urban and road noise and preserving a secluded, peaceful atmosphere within the metropolitan area.

Intact historic estates of this scale and character within the Lisbon metropolitan area are extremely rare and virtually never offered off-market. 02 — Character & fabric

An ensemble of integrity.

The estate retains the classic typology of a south-facing Portuguese quinta: principal residence with distinctive blue-and-white elevations, agricultural and service wings, a chapel of the 17th/18th century, mature formal gardens, reflecting pools and productive land.

Ancillary structures include the historic lagar, adega and former agricultural buildings. The overall composition offers rare continuity between architecture, landscape and history — an urban oasis that has endured the expansion of Greater Lisbon.

03 — Location

Metropolitan access.
Private scale.

The estate occupies a privileged position on the south bank of the Tagus. Exceptional connectivity to Lisbon’s financial and cultural core and to the international airport is combined with a level of privacy and acoustic seclusion more commonly associated with rural estates.

The mature perimeter planting of Australian-origin trees creates a natural sound buffer that isolates the interior of the property from surrounding urban activity, reinforcing its character as a discreet and peaceful enclave.
